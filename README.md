# Thesis Management System (TMS)

## 1. Introduction
In most universities, the thesis process is still handled manually. Proposals, supervisor feedback, deadlines and final submissions move through emails, paper forms and personal follow-ups. This makes the process slow, hard to track and difficult to manage for everyone involved.

The Thesis Management System (TMS) is a web-based platform that brings the complete thesis life-cycle into one place, from proposal submission to final approval and archiving. This document presents the business case, scope and expected outcomes of the proposed system.

## 2. Problem Definition
The current manual process has the following problems:

- Proposals and drafts are shared by email or hard copy, so documents get lost or delayed.
- There is no clear version history, so supervisors and students work on different drafts.
- Feedback is slow and scattered across emails, messages and verbal comments.
- Coordinators cannot easily track the progress or deadlines of every student.
- There is no central record of past theses, which leads to repeated or duplicate topics.

As a result, the department faces delays, miscommunication and poor record keeping.

## 3. Stakeholders and Their Interests

- Student: Easy submission of proposal and drafts, clear feedback, deadline tracking
- Supervisor: Review drafts, give feedback, monitor student progress
- Thesis Coordinator: Assign supervisors, schedule defenses, track overall status
- Evaluation Committee: Access the final thesis and submit evaluation marks
- Department / University: Organized records, plagiarism control, better research quality
- System Administrator: User management, security and system maintenance

## 4. Vision
To provide a single, reliable and transparent platform where students, supervisors and administrators can manage thesis work efficiently, reducing delays and paperwork while improving research quality and record keeping.

## 5. Scope

### 5.1 High Level Features

1. User Management: Registration and role-based login for student, supervisor, coordinator, committee member and admin
2. Proposal Submission: Student submits thesis proposal for approval
3. Draft Management: Upload drafts with version history
4. Feedback System: Supervisor gives feedback on drafts
5. Deadline Tracking: Track deadlines and progress of every student
6. Defense Scheduling: Coordinator schedules defenses
7. Evaluation: Committee accesses final thesis and submits marks
8. Archiving: Central record of past thesis
